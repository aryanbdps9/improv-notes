- Defined and discussed `CROW(E)` which is what improvisers are trying to establish during scenes:

    ```
    Character
    Relationship
    Objective
    Where
    ...bonus: Emotion
    ```

- Practiced the C of Crow with playing with walking different ways leading with different body parts and influenced by different animals

- **Thank You Endowment** - One person stands in the middle and poses. Everyone else take turns giving one line of dialogue, trying to include as much of CROW as possible in the one line. The person making the pose says "Thank you", because it's a gift to have your offer be given meaning

- **Corridors**: Standing in 2 lines, the front pair does a 3 line scene trying to establish CROW in those three lines.

- **Freeze Tag**: 2 improvisers start a scene and we say "freeze" when CROW has been established. The 2 improvisers in the scene freeze, then the person who said freeze taps one of them out and assumes their physical position. They then begin a new scene with a new CROW justifying the physical position that they are in.

- **Focus Count**: Focusing on focal point the group sees how high we can count without people saying the same number. When that happens, we applaud wildly for our failure and then start back again at one.

- **Compliment Circle**: Told the person we were watching one thing they did well in class today.

- **It's Tuesday!**: One person says, "It's Tuesday!" and the other person continues. Dull is okay!
